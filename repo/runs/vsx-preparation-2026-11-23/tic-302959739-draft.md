## Proposed remarks in English

> Variability of TIC 302959739 (= 2MASS J09042304-7137155; Gaia DR3 5221973868734158976) was previously reported in the flare study of Feinstein et al. (2024, AJ, 168, 60; doi:10.3847/1538-3881/ad4edf). The paper-linked final flare catalog contains 37 events assigned to this TIC. No claim of first discovery is made here.
>
> The authors' public period table lists Prot = 0.437769157672309 d and e_Prot = 0.00427381457161783 d, with Good = 0. Their rotation-analysis script selects Good = 1. This period is therefore cited as a previously published candidate estimate, outside that selected subset, and is not adopted as a secure rotation period. The quoted e_Prot is the table value, not an uncertainty measured by our project.
>
> Our reanalysis of archival TESS SPOC light curves finds a recurring photometric timescale near 0.4377 d in six of seven PDCSAP sectors (10, 11, 12, 37, 38 and 39). Sector 12 retains competing aliases. Unmodified Sector 36 fails our repeatability criterion. A post-hoc mask of 114 out of 15,679 valid cadences restores a signal near 0.437909 d in both SAP and PDCSAP for that sector; this diagnostic does not replace the original failure. The original Sector 39 SAP preference remains 6.567413 d. The combined frozen adjudication remains unresolved, with no adopted final period.
>
> Exploratory PRF fits in sectors 10, 39 and 36 favor a location near the target. A Gaia neighbor (source_id 5221973868731579136), approximately 5.09 arcsec away and 1.97 mag fainter in G, has not been conclusively excluded. The location offsets are not calibrated astrometric uncertainties. SkyMapper DR4 identifies the two sources separately, but sparse and partly blended epoch photometry has not established the origin of the periodic variation.
>
> The physical variability type, intrinsic amplitude, period uncertainty and reference epoch remain undetermined. TESS times are retained as BTJD = BJD_TDB − 2457000. No HJD epoch or calibrated magnitude range is supplied. The prior flare reports do not by themselves resolve the source or physical interpretation of the recurring modulation in our analysis.
